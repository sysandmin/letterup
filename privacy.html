Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Letter Up is a word game for iPad. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. The short version: nothing leaves your iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. Letter Up doesn't collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your iPad

To remember your games and choices, Letter Up saves a few things on your iPad only:

  • names you type for players, and the colours you pick
  • your settings, such as level, spelling (US or UK) and appearance
  • games in progress, so you can pick up where you left off

This information never leaves your device. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Letter Up is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Beta testing

If you test Letter Up through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us. That's handled by Apple under the TestFlight terms and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that needs data, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
