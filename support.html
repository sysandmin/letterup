Support

Letter Up is a word game for one or two players on one iPad. Get from the first word to the goal word by changing one letter at a time. Every step has to be a real word.

Need help? Email letterup.app@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap a letter, then type a new one. The new word has to be a real word, and you can't repeat a word you've already used. Tap ? on the Home screen for the full rules.

How do I change my name or colour?

On the setup screen, tap your name (or the ✎). Your name and the colours open right there. Changes save as you type.

Can I stop a game I don't want to finish?

Yes. On the Home screen, tap the ⓧ next to Resume and confirm.

US or UK spelling?

Pick on the setup screen before you start. Words like COLOR and COLOUR follow the spelling you chose.

A real word wasn't accepted.

Our word lists leave out very rare words and offensive words. If you think we missed a normal word, email us the word and we'll look at it.

Does Letter Up need the internet?

No. It works offline and nothing leaves your iPad. See our Privacy Policy.
